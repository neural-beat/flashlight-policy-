Privacy Policy — Flashlight

Last updated: August 2026

Flashlight is developed by Neural Beat ("we", "us"). This policy explains what happens to your data when you use the app.

What the app does

Flashlight turns your device's camera flash on and off, or — on devices without a flash — lights up the screen instead, with adjustable brightness and a strobe/SOS mode. All of this happens entirely on your device.

Data we collect

Flashlight itself does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. We do not ask for your name, email, location, or any account information, and the app does not require camera or storage permissions to work (turning the flash on and off does not require the camera permission on Android).

Advertising

The app shows ads served by Google AdMob, which is how we keep the app free to use. AdMob may collect information such as advertising identifiers, device information, and approximate location to show and measure ads, and may use this data according to its own policies. You can review how Google handles this data in Google's Privacy Policy and manage ad personalization in your device's Google settings.

Data sharing and sale

We do not sell your data. We do not share any data with third parties beyond what is described above for ad delivery through Google AdMob.

Children's privacy

Flashlight is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy if the app changes. Updates will be posted at this same address.

Contact
